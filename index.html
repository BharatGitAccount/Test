VIJAY VALLABH ENTERPRISES

Construction materials and waterproofing solutions

Vijay Vallabh Enterprises is a Mumbai-based distributor, supplier and trader of construction materials. Its publicly listed product categories include waterproofing chemicals, cement, wall putty, tile and stone adhesives, repair polymers, micro-concrete, coatings, mortar and other building materials.

Company profile

Established around 2010, the company is publicly listed as operating from Lower Parel, Mumbai. Its current business activity centres on supplying construction chemicals and building materials for waterproofing, repair, masonry, surface preparation and finishing requirements.
